## About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Astrophysics Division (BP4) of the
[National Centre for Nuclear Research](https://www.ncbj.gov.pl/en) in Warsaw,
and an affiliated researcher at INAF – Osservatorio Astronomico di Brera in Milan.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics with distinction from NCBJ in 2023, as a member of
the group studying the formation and evolution of galaxies. From 2024 to 2026 I was
a postdoctoral researcher in the Physics Department of the Università degli Studi
di Milano-Bicocca.

## Research

I work mainly with spectroscopic data from large galaxy and quasar surveys.

- **Machine learning for quasar spectra.** I develop neural-network methods for the
  automatic analysis of quasar spectra, such as continuum estimation and the detection
  of metal absorption lines, for the WEAVE-QSO survey.
- **Chemical evolution of galaxies.** During my Ph.D. I studied how galaxies evolve
  through the chemical enrichment of their interstellar medium, and how this connects
  to their other physical properties, using SDSS data in the local Universe and VIPERS
  data at intermediate redshift (z ∼ 0.7).
