About me

I am a postdoctoral researcher in the Astrophysics Division (BP4) of the National Centre for Nuclear Research in Warsaw, and an affiliated researcher at INAF – Osservatorio Astronomico di Brera in Milan.

I received my Ph.D. in Physics with distinction from NCBJ in 2023, as a member of the group studying the formation and evolution of galaxies. From 2024 to 2026 I was a postdoctoral researcher in the Physics Department of the Università degli Studi di Milano-Bicocca.

Research

I work mainly with spectroscopic data from large galaxy and quasar surveys.

Machine learning for quasar spectra

Neural networks for the automatic analysis of quasar spectra, such as continuum estimation and the detection of metal absorption lines, for the WEAVE-QSO survey.

Related papers →

Chemical evolution of galaxies

How galaxies are enriched with metals, and how this links to their mass and star formation: the fundamental metallicity relation from SDSS (z ∼ 0) to VIPERS (z ∼ 0.7).
